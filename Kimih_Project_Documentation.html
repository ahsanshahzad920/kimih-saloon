Version 2.0 • Production Ready

1. Executive Summary

Kimih is a modern, high-performance beauty, wellness, and salon booking marketplace platform. It connects customers seeking premium beauty services with top-rated salons, spas, and independent beauty professionals. The system provides a seamless experience for end-users to discover, compare, and book appointments online while empowering salon partners with management software for staff, schedules, bookings, and inventory.
